This archive is a sanitized source package reconstructed from the uploaded project ZIP.

IMPORTANT: This is NOT represented as a security-fixed production release. Source review found

Do not deploy publicly until these are fixed and tested. Preserve fallback settings:
FBRAH_ALLOW_LOCAL_MODEL_FALLBACK=1
FBRAH_ALLOW_CLOUD_FALLBACK=1

The archive intentionally does not include secrets or private data. Restore local .env and databases
only from trusted local backups after rotating credentials and session secrets.
